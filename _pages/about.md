I am an economic consultant specialising in quantitative impact modelling, industrial organisation, and technology economics. My work spans defence, public sector, and global technology clients — producing rigorous economic analysis that informs strategy, policy, and litigation.

My technical foundation is in econometrics and causal inference, developed through a PhD in Economics at the University of Geneva. I no longer work in academic research, but I bring that analytical rigour into applied consulting and independent writing.
