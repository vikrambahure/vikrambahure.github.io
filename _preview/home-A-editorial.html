Who is paying for AI?

The build-out has quietly shifted from cash to credit, and the credit is increasingly hard to see through.

By Vikram Bahure  ·  29 May 2026  ·  Research / Macro

The companies building artificial intelligence have started to borrow to pay for it. For most of the past decade the five big hyperscalers funded their data-centre spending entirely from operating cash flow. In 2026 that reverses: capital spending will roughly equal operating cash flow, on a reported bill of about $646bn.

The question is no longer how much cash Big Tech throws off. It is who is lending it the difference, and how sound that lending is.
