This is a place for serious writing.

I am an economist by training — development economics, political economy, the messy intersection of institutions and human behaviour. Over the years I have accumulated opinions, half-formed arguments, and research threads that do not fit neatly into academic papers. This blog is where those go.

**What you will find here:**

- **Finance & Markets** — analysis of financial trends, monetary policy, and capital markets through an economist's lens
- **Geopolitics** — the economics of power, conflict, and international order
- **Development** — field research, policy debates, and ideas from the frontiers of development economics
- **Research Notes** — technical notes, paper summaries, and academic commentary for readers who want the details

I write for people who think carefully. No oversimplification, no hot takes dressed as analysis. If something takes three paragraphs to explain properly, it gets three paragraphs.
